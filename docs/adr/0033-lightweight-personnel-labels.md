# Use lightweight Personnel Labels instead of a beta template hierarchy

Beta represents personnel as an optional searchable label on a Play. Formation role slots and the Play's actual Players remain authoritative, avoiding a third reusable template system alongside Concepts and Formations.

## Consequences

- Built-in suggestions include common offensive, defensive, and special-teams labels such as `10`, `11`, `12`, `21`, `Nickel`, `Dime`, `Goal Line`, `Punt`, and `Field Goal`.
- Coaches may create, rename, or apply any custom Personnel Label.
- Personnel is optional and never required below Unit.
- Formation role slots and Play Players determine actual composition; a Personnel Label does not add, remove, rename, or reposition Players.
- Chalk may derive and display position-group counts from the Play's role-based Players.
- Personnel Labels participate in filtering, position sheets, and static outputs.
- Applying or changing a label never triggers template reapplication or geometry changes.
- A reusable Personnel entity is deferred until observed workflows establish a need that Formations and labels cannot satisfy.
